
The TechFreqs Faster Forge mod V1.0, that supercharges the forge's tools (Anvil, Bellows, and Crucible) to make smelting and crafting extremely fast—bordering on overpowered (OP) for a grind-free playstyle. It speeds up all forge recipes (no matter what you're making), focusing on equipping the tools to halve (or more) the time for both smelting (e.g., melting scrap into ingots) and crafting (e.g., making forged iron/steel). Perfect for players who want quick progression without waiting around, while keeping the game "pretty fast" and fun.

Key Features:
Speed Boosts: Each tool reduces forge times by 90% (10x faster, or 10% of base time) for both smelting (CraftingSmeltTime) and crafting (CraftingTime). This applies globally to any recipe in the forge—no ties to specific items.

Anvil: Boosts crafting and adds smelting speedup (removes a vanilla requirement so it always works when equipped).
Bellows: Boosts smelting and adds crafting speedup.
Crucible: Adds speedups to both (vanilla crucible doesn't affect speed, but this mods it in).

Stacking Effects: The bonuses multiply when tools are equipped together in the forge:
1 tool: ~10x faster.
2 tools: ~100x faster.
All 3 tools: ~1000x faster (basically instant—e.g., smelting a stack or crafting steel happens in a split second).

UI Updates: Tooltips show the speedup (e.g., "0.9" in your XML, but ideally tweak to "0.1" for accuracy to reflect the 10% time multiplier).

Other Details:
No changes to recipes, outputs, or fuel use—just pure speed.
Builds on vanilla mechanics but amps them up (e.g., vanilla anvil/bellows only mildly speed up one aspect; this makes them OP all-rounders).
Safe and reversible: Uninstall by deleting the mod folder; saves/worlds remain intact, reverting to vanilla speeds.
